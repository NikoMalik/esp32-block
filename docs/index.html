Flash esp32-block

A tiny DNS ad blocker for the whole network, on a $5 ESP32. Plug the board into USB and flash it straight from this page -> no toolchain, no app.

Your browser can't talk to serial devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop.

Click Install, pick your board's serial port, and let it run. If no port shows up, hold the BOOT button while connecting. After it finishes, the box joins your WiFi from your phone (captive portal) -> see the README.

What gets written (classic ESP32, 4 MB flash):

bootloader0x1000
partition table0x8000
boot app selector0xe000
firmware0x10000
blocklist (~645k domains)0x170000
